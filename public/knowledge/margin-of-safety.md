# Margin Of Safety

## Summary
The margin of safety is a stock market pricing concept that refers to the difference between a stock's intrinsic value and its market price. It is also used in accounting to describe the difference between expected sales and the break-even point. The concept was coined by Benjamin Graham and David Dodd, founders of value investing. The idea is to buy stocks with a significant margin of safety to protect against poor decisions and market downturns.

## Key concepts
- Intrinsic value vs market price
- Break-even analysis
- Value investing philosophy
- Benjamin Graham and David Dodd
- Warren Buffett's bridge analogy
- 90% of intrinsic value for high-quality issues
