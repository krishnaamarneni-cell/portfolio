# Moat Concept

## Summary
A moat refers to a business's durable competitive advantage, enabling it to sustain returns above the cost of capital. Common moat sources include brand, network effects, and regulatory barriers. Stocks with wide moats tend to compound owner earnings with less volatility, but often at the cost of slower growth.

## Key concepts
- Durable competitive advantage
- Brand recognition
- Network effects
- High switching costs
- Regulatory barriers

As Krishna has a significant portion of his portfolio in NKE (Nike), which is known for its strong brand recognition, understanding the concept of a moat can help him evaluate the durability of Nike's competitive advantage and its potential impact on his investment.
