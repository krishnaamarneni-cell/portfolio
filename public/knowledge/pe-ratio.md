# Price Earnings Ratio

## Summary
The price-earnings ratio is a financial metric used to value companies and determine if they are overvalued or undervalued. It is calculated by dividing a company's share price by its earnings per share. A lower P/E ratio indicates that a company is undervalued, while a higher ratio suggests it is overvalued. The average P/E ratio for the S&P 500 index has ranged from 4.78 to 44.20 since 1900.
